RECITALS

WHEREAS: The Grantor is the competent authority responsible for the development and regulation of Vietnam's maritime infrastructure;
WHEREAS: The Concessionaire has been selected through international competitive bidding to develop, finance, and operate the Can Gio International Transshipment Port;
WHEREAS: The parties wish to enter into this Concession Agreement to govern the terms and conditions of the 30-year concession;

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

TermDefinition
"Concession Area"Approximately 185 hectares as described in Schedule 1
"Concession Term"30 years from the Commercial Operations Date
"Development Cost"$5.5 Billion USD (estimated)
"Commercial Operations Date"As defined in Article 5

ARTICLE 2: GRANT OF CONCESSION

2.1 The Grantor hereby grants to the Concessionaire the exclusive right to develop, finance, design, construct, operate, maintain, and manage the Can Gio International Transshipment Port within the Concession Area for the Concession Term.
2.2 The concession includes the right to:
  • Construct and operate up to 6 deep-water berths (18m draft)
  • Develop container handling facilities of up to 15M TEU annual capacity
  • Establish and operate logistics and warehousing facilities
  • Charge and collect fees, tariffs, and other charges

ARTICLE 3: CONCESSION TERM

3.1 The Concession Term shall commence on the Commercial Operations Date and continue for a period of thirty (30) years.
3.2 The Concessionaire shall have the right to renew the Concession Term for additional periods, subject to mutual agreement and fulfillment of all obligations.
