RECITALS

WHEREAS: The Grantor is the competent authority for maritime infrastructure under Vietnamese law;

WHEREAS: The Concessionaire was selected as the investment consortium following the Prime Minister’s approval of the investment policy on January 16, 2025, and the Ho Chi Minh City People’s Committee’s issuance of the investor approval on April 13, 2026;

WHEREAS: The Parties now wish to formalise the terms and conditions of the 50‑year concession.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

PARTIES TO THIS AGREEMENT

THE GRANTOR

MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT OF THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM
Established under Decree No. 12/2017/ND‑CP dated 10 February 2017
Principal office: 80 Trần Hưng Đạo, Hoàn Kiếm District, Hanoi, Vietnam

Represented by: H.E. Nguyễn Văn Thắng, Minister of Transport

THE CONCESSIONAIRE CONSORTIUM

Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A. (Switzerland) – 49%
Vietnam Maritime Corporation (VIMC) (State‑owned, Vietnam) – 36%
Saigon Port Joint Stock Company (Vietnam) – 15%

Represented by: Capt. Alessandro Del Vecchio (TIL), and authorised representatives of VIMC and Saigon Port.

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

  • “Agreement” means this Concession Agreement together with all Schedules.
  • “Commercial Operations Date (COD)” means the date certified by the Grantor following completion of Phase 2 and successful trial operations, as further defined in Article 5.3.
  • “Concession Area” means approximately 571 hectares including Go Con Cho Islet at the mouth of the Cai Mep River, as described in Schedule 1.
  • “Concession Fee” means the annual fixed fee payable by the Concessionaire to the Grantor under Article 5.1(a).
  • “Concession Term” means the period specified in Article 3.1.
  • “Consortium Member” means each of TIL, VIMC, and Saigon Port.
  • “CPI” means the Consumer Price Index for Vietnam published by the General Statistics Office.
  • “Development Cost” means VND 128.872 trillion (approximately US$4.9 billion).
  • “Event of Default” has the meaning given in Article 14.
  • “Force Majeure” means any event described in Article 13.2.
  • “Grantor” means the Ministry of Transport of Vietnam.
  • “Lenders” means the financial institutions providing debt financing for the Project.
  • “Liquidated Damages” means the amounts set out in Article 6.2.
  • “Project” means the development, financing, design, construction, operation, maintenance, and management of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port.
  • “Revenue Share” means the percentage of gross revenues payable to the Grantor under Article 5.1(b).
  • “Step‑In Right” means the right of Lenders under Article 11.4.
  • “Termination Compensation” means the amount payable under Article 15.5.

1.2 Interpretation
(a) Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.
(b) “Includes” or “including” means “including without limitation”.
(c) Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
(d) References to “US$” or “dollars” mean United States dollars.

ARTICLE 2: GRANT OF CONCESSION

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Grantor hereby grants to the Concessionaire the exclusive right to develop, finance, design, construct, operate, maintain, and manage the Project within the Concession Area for the Concession Term.

2.2 The concession includes the right to:
(a) construct and operate up to 13 deep‑water berths, with the first phase including two to four berths capable of handling vessels of up to 250,000 metric tons (equivalent to 24,000 TEU class vessels);
(b) develop container handling facilities with an annual capacity of 4.8 million TEU by 2030, scaling up to 16.9 million TEU by 2047;
(c) establish and operate logistics and warehousing facilities within the Concession Area;
(d) charge and collect fees, tariffs, and other charges from port users in accordance with Schedule 3;
(e) enter into sub‑concession or operation and maintenance agreements with third parties, subject to the Grantor’s prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

2.3 The Grantor reserves the right to regulate maritime safety, security, and environmental standards applicable to the Project.

ARTICLE 3: CONCESSION TERM

3.1 The Concession Term shall commence on the Commercial Operations Date and continue for a period of fifty (50) years, unless earlier terminated under Article 14 or Article 15.

3.2 The Concessionaire may request renewal of the Concession Term for one or more additional periods, each of up to ten (10) years. Any renewal shall be subject to mutual written agreement of the Parties, the Concessionaire having fulfilled all material obligations under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 4: DEVELOPMENT OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Concessionaire shall develop the Project in accordance with the master plan set out in Schedule 2, at a total Development Cost of no less than VND 128.872 trillion (approximately US$4.9 billion), unless otherwise agreed by the Grantor.

4.2 The Project shall be developed in seven phases:
- Phase 1: Construction of initial berths, ground‑breaking scheduled for April 30, 2026, targeted operational readiness by 2027.
- Phases 2 to 7: Subsequent phases to be completed progressively, with the final phase targeted for 2040.

4.3 The Consortium Members shall directly contribute 15% of the total investment as equity, with the remainder raised from other sources, including debt financing.

4.4 The Concessionaire is barred from transferring the Project for a period of ten (10) years from the date of land handover by the Grantor.

4.5 The Concessionaire must disburse at least VND 50 trillion (approximately US$1.9 billion) within the first ten (10) years of the Concession Term.

4.6 The entire Project shall be completed within twenty (20) years from the Commercial Operations Date.

4.7 All construction shall comply with Vietnamese national technical regulations (QCVN) and internationally recognised standards (PIANC, ISO, IMO).

ARTICLE 5: FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

5.1 Payments to the Grantor
(a) Concession Fee: The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor an annual Concession Fee as set out in Schedule 3. The first payment shall be due on the Commercial Operations Date. Subsequent payments shall be due annually on each anniversary of the COD. The Concession Fee shall be escalated annually by the percentage change in the CPI plus 2% (two percent), applied on each anniversary.
(b) Revenue Share: The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor a Revenue Share equal to the percentage of gross revenues set out in Schedule 3 (initially 5% of gross revenues). Payment shall be made quarterly within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter.

5.2 Payment Procedures: All payments shall be made in United States dollars or Vietnamese Dong as specified in Schedule 3, by wire transfer to a bank account designated by the Ministry of Finance of Vietnam. The Concessionaire shall provide a payment confirmation to the Grantor within five (5) business days of each transfer.

5.3 Audit Rights: The Grantor (or its authorised auditor) may, upon 15 business days’ written notice and not more than once per calendar year, audit the Concessionaire’s books and records to verify revenue share and other financial obligations. The cost of any audit that reveals an underpayment exceeding 3% shall be borne by the Concessionaire.

5.4 Accounting Standards: The Concessionaire shall maintain its accounts in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and Vietnamese accounting standards (VAS) where not inconsistent with IFRS.

5.5 Reporting: Within 60 days after the end of each fiscal year, the Concessionaire shall deliver to the Grantor audited financial statements (balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement) for the Project.

6.1 The Concessionaire shall achieve the following milestones:

6.2 If the Concessionaire fails to meet any milestone by its deadline, it shall pay the applicable liquidated damages for each calendar day of delay until the milestone is achieved. Such payments shall be made monthly to the Grantor.

6.3 Liquidated damages shall not exceed in aggregate 10% of the total Development Cost. No liquidated damages shall accrue during any period of Force Majeure.

ARTICLE 7: OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE

The Concessionaire shall operate and maintain the Project in accordance with internationally recognised port operation standards (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001), maintain adequate staffing, and submit an annual maintenance plan to the Grantor.

ARTICLE 8: TARIFFS AND FEE COLLECTION

All tariffs charged to port users shall be set forth in Schedule 3. Any proposed tariff adjustment exceeding CPI + 3% in any 12‑month period shall require the Grantor’s prior written approval, not to be unreasonably withheld.

ARTICLE 9: ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL OBLIGATIONS

The Concessionaire shall comply with all Vietnamese environmental laws (Law on Environmental Protection 2020), obtain all necessary environmental permits, and implement the Environmental Management Plan set out in Schedule 5. Any material environmental damage shall be remediated at the Concessionaire’s cost.

ARTICLE 10: INSURANCE

The Concessionaire shall maintain, at its own cost, the following insurance: (a) all‑risk property insurance (replacement value); (b) third‑party liability (minimum US$200 million per occurrence); (c) construction all‑risk during development; (d) business interruption. Evidence of insurance shall be provided to the Grantor annually.

ARTICLE 11: FINANCING AND STEP‑IN RIGHTS

11.1 The Concessionaire shall secure all necessary debt and equity financing for the Development Cost of VND 128.872 trillion.

11.2 The Consortium Members shall directly contribute 15% of the total investment as equity, with the remainder raised from other sources, including debt financing.

11.3 The Grantor shall have no liability for any financing obtained by the Concessionaire.

11.4 The Concessionaire may grant security over its rights under this Agreement to its Lenders.

11.5 Step‑In Rights: Upon an Event of Default by the Concessionaire that would otherwise entitle the Grantor to terminate this Agreement, the Lenders shall have the right (but not the obligation) to “step in” and cure the default within 90 days, provided they give written notice to the Grantor. If the Lenders cure the default, the Grantor shall not terminate.

ARTICLE 12: CHANGE IN LAW AND POLITICAL RISK

12.1 If a change in Vietnamese law after the effective date of this Agreement imposes materially increased costs on the Concessionaire (including taxes, fees, or operational restrictions), the Grantor shall compensate the Concessionaire for such additional costs, or alternatively the Parties shall renegotiate the financial terms of this Agreement in good faith.

12.2 Expropriation, nationalisation, or unlawful revocation of the concession by the Grantor shall entitle the Concessionaire to compensation equal to fair market value plus 15%.

ARTICLE 13: FORCE MAJEURE

13.1 “Force Majeure” means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, including: war, invasion, armed conflict, terrorism, nuclear or chemical contamination, natural disasters (flood, earthquake, typhoon), epidemic/pandemic declared by WHO, strikes, or any order of a court or governmental authority that is not due to the affected Party’s non‑compliance.

13.2 A Party affected by Force Majeure shall promptly notify the other Party. Performance obligations shall be suspended for the duration of the Force Majeure event. If Force Majeure continues for more than 180 days, either Party may terminate this Agreement without liability.

ARTICLE 14: EVENTS OF DEFAULT

Each of the following constitutes an Event of Default:
(a) Failure to pay any Concession Fee or Revenue Share when due, if not cured within 30 days of written notice;
(b) Material breach of any other obligation under this Agreement, if not cured within 60 days of written notice;
(c) The Concessionaire becoming insolvent, entering liquidation, or having a receiver appointed;
(d) The Concessionaire abandoning the Project for more than 90 consecutive days;
(e) A material misrepresentation in any certification provided to the Grantor.

ARTICLE 15: TERMINATION AND COMPENSATION ON TERMINATION

15.1 Termination for Concessionaire Default
If the Grantor terminates for an Event of Default by the Concessionaire, the Concessionaire shall forfeit all rights under this Agreement. Termination Compensation shall be calculated as the lower of (i) fair market value of the Project assets or (ii) 50% of the net book value of the Concessionaire’s investment.

15.2 Termination for Grantor Default
If the Concessionaire terminates due to the Grantor’s material breach (including unlawful expropriation), the Grantor shall pay Termination Compensation equal to the fair market value of the Project plus 20% of that value as additional damages.

15.3 Termination for Force Majeure
If terminated under Article 13.2, neither Party shall owe compensation, but the Concessionaire shall be entitled to removal of its movable assets.

15.4 Step‑In Rights override
No termination shall be effective while Lenders are exercising Step‑In Rights under Article 11.5.

ARTICLE 16: DISPUTE RESOLUTION – ARBITRATION

16.1 Negotiation
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiations between senior executives.

16.2 Arbitration
If not resolved within 60 days, the dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its rules in force at the time.
- Number of arbitrators: three (3).
- Place of arbitration: Singapore.
- Language: English.
- The arbitral award shall be final and binding and may be enforced in any court of competent jurisdiction.

16.3 Exclusion of other forums
The Parties irrevocably waive any right to bring proceedings in any national court, except for enforcement of an arbitral award.

ARTICLE 17: GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, except that arbitration procedure shall be governed by the SIAC rules and the law of Singapore.

ARTICLE 18: CONFIDENTIALITY

Neither Party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement to any third party without the other Party’s prior written consent, except as required by law, stock exchange rules, or to its professional advisers, Lenders, or insurers under a duty of confidentiality.

ARTICLE 19: ASSIGNMENT

The Concessionaire may not assign this Agreement or any material right hereunder without the Grantor’s prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a wholly‑owned affiliate or to the Lenders as security.

ARTICLE 20: INDEMNIFICATION

20.1 The Concessionaire shall indemnify and hold harmless the Grantor from any third‑party claims, damages, or liabilities arising from the Concessionaire’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct in developing or operating the Project.

20.2 The Grantor shall indemnify the Concessionaire from any claims arising from the Grantor’s breach of its regulatory obligations or unlawful acts.

ARTICLE 21: NOTICES

All formal notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set out in the preamble (or as updated by written notice). Notices shall be deemed received: (a) if delivered by hand, on delivery; (b) if sent by registered post, 5 business days after posting; (c) if by email with confirmed receipt, on the next business day.

ARTICLE 22: MISCELLANEOUS

22.1 This Agreement (including the Schedules) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations.

22.2 Only a written instrument signed by both Parties may amend this Agreement.

22.3 If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in full force.

22.4 Failure to enforce any right shall not constitute a waiver.

22.5 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
