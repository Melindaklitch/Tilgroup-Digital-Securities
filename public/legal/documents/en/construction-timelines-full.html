1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "COD" means Commercial Operations Date as defined in Article 1 of the Concession Agreement.
  • "Phase 1 Completion" means the date on which dredging and land reclamation for the entire Concession Area (571 hectares) is certified as complete by the independent engineer.
  • "Phase 2 Completion" means the date on which Berths 1–4 are constructed and operational.
  • "Final Completion" means the date on which all 13 berths and associated infrastructure are complete and the Project reaches full design capacity (16.9 million TEU per annum).
  • "Independent Engineer" means an internationally recognised engineering firm appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire.

2.1 The Project shall be developed in five (5) phases as summarised below. Detailed milestones and binding deadlines are set out in Articles 3 through 8.

Key Milestone: Channel dredging complete by 31 December 2026. Contractor: Boskalis Westminster. Liquidated damages for delay: US$50,000 per day (as per Schedule 3 of the Concession Agreement).

4. PHASE 2 – BERTH CONSTRUCTION (BERTHS 1–4)

ActivityDeadlineStatus
Piling and foundation work – Berths 1–230 June 2026In progress (15% complete)
Superstructure – Berths 1–231 December 2027Planned
Piling and foundation – Berths 3–431 March 2027Planned
Superstructure – Berths 3–431 December 2028Planned

4.1 Berths 1–2 shall have a minimum quay length of 400m each and a draft of 18m. Upon completion of Berths 1–2, the Concessionaire shall achieve an initial handling capacity of 5 million TEU per annum.

5. PHASE 3 – TERMINAL INFRASTRUCTURE

5.1 By 30 June 2028, the Concessionaire shall complete: container yard development (minimum 100 hectares paved), warehouse facilities, administrative buildings, and all utility connections (power, water, telecommunications).

6. PHASE 4 – TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION

6.1 By 31 March 2029, the Concessionaire shall install and commission: automated stacking cranes (ASC), terminal operating system (TOS), IoT sensor network, and a cybersecurity infrastructure compliant with ISO 27001.

7. PHASE 5 – OPERATIONAL READINESS AND COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS DATE (COD)

7.1 By 31 December 2030, the Concessionaire shall achieve: completion of staff training, successful system testing, commissioning of all berths (minimum 4 berths operational), and receipt of the COD certificate from the Grantor.

7.2 The COD shall be the date from which the Concession Term (50 years) commences under Article 3 of the Concession Agreement.

9. RISK ALLOCATION AND MITIGATION

9.1 The following risks are allocated as set out in the Concession Agreement (Articles 12, 13, and 14). This Schedule provides specific mitigation measures:

RiskMitigation
Weather delays (typhoons, monsoons)Schedule buffer of three (3) months incorporated into each phase; all-weather dredging equipment.
Supply chain / material availabilityDual‑source contracts for critical materials (cement, steel).
Permitting delaysAll permits for Phases 1–2 secured as of April 2026; Grantor to use reasonable endeavours for subsequent permits.
Labour shortagesTraining programmes for local workforce; international contractor support from Boskalis and CHEC.

10. REPORTING AND AUDIT RIGHTS

10.1 The Concessionaire shall deliver quarterly progress reports to the Grantor within 15 business days after the end of each calendar quarter. Each report shall include:

  • Actual vs planned milestone status
  • Photographic documentation of key works
  • Budget vs actual expenditure (cumulative)
  • Updated risk register

10.2 The Grantor (or its authorised auditor) may inspect the construction site upon 48 hours’ notice, at no cost to the Concessionaire, up to four times per calendar year.

11. ADOPTION OF SCHEDULE

This Schedule 2 is attached to and forms an integral part of the Concession Agreement dated April 2026 between the Ministry of Transport of Vietnam and the Concessionaire Consortium (TIL, VIMC, Saigon Port).

The Parties acknowledge and agree to be bound by the milestones, deadlines, and liquidated damages set out in this Schedule.
