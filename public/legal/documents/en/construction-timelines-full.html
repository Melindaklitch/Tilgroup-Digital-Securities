5.0-7.0 Phase Overview: Terminal Infrastructure to Operational Readiness

🏭 Phase 3: Terminal Infrastructure (2026-2028)

Container yard development, warehouse construction, administrative buildings, and utility infrastructure. Target completion: Q2 2028.

🤖 Phase 4: Technology Integration (2027-2029)

Automated stacking cranes, terminal operating system (TOS) implementation, IoT sensor network, and cybersecurity infrastructure.

✅ Phase 5: Operational Readiness (2028-2030)

Staff training, system testing, commissioning, and phased operational handover. Commercial operations commencement targeted for Q4 2030.
