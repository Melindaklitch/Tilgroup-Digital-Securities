1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "COD" means Commercial Operations Date as defined in the Concession Agreement.
  • "Phase 1 Completion" means the date on which dredging and land reclamation for the areas specified in the approved engineering design and investment project documents are certified as complete by the Independent Engineer.
  • "Phase 2 Completion" means the date on which Berths 1–4 are constructed and operational (subject to the phased schedule in Article 6).
  • "Final Completion" means the date on which all berths and associated infrastructure are complete and the Project reaches its ultimate design capacity, as defined in the investment approval and the Concession Agreement.
  • "Independent Engineer" has the meaning given in Article 14 of this Schedule.
  • "Effective Date" means the date of execution of the Concession Agreement.

2. CONTRACTING PARTY AND INCORPORATION

2.1 The Parties acknowledge that the Concessionaire is the Project Company, which shall be incorporated (or has been incorporated) in accordance with the provisions of the Concession Agreement.

2.2 The exact legal name, jurisdiction of incorporation, registered address, and shareholding structure of the Project Company shall be set out in the Concession Agreement. If the Project Company has not been incorporated prior to the Effective Date, the Concession Agreement shall specify the mechanism by which the Consortium Members shall establish it and transfer/novate the concession rights.

Note: The following structures are possible and must be confirmed in the main agreement:

  • Structure A: Project Company already incorporated – identify by exact legal name, registration number, and registered address.
  • Structure B: Consortium members sign initially, with an obligation to incorporate and novate/transfer to the Project Company.
  • Structure C: Special‑purpose vehicle incorporated before execution – name it directly.

This Schedule assumes that the contracting party is the Project Company, but the actual structure shall be governed by the Concession Agreement.

5. PHASE 1 – DREDGING AND LAND RECLAMATION

5.1 The Concessionaire shall complete the remaining dredging and reclamation works in accordance with the approved engineering design and investment project documents. Delays shall be subject to liquidated damages as provided in the Concession Agreement (Articles 6 and Schedule 3).

ActivityDeadlineRemarks
Channel dredging – completion of remaining works31 December 2026To achieve the design depth specified in the approved marine engineering design.
Land reclamation – completion of areas specified in the approved documents31 December 2026Including sediment disposal and environmental monitoring.

Key Milestone: Dredging and reclamation complete by 31 December 2026. The works shall be procured by the Concessionaire in accordance with the procurement and contractor requirements of the Concession Agreement.

Note: Based on current project information, the overall project area is approximately 571 hectares. The exact reclamation scope and dredging volumes (e.g., approximately 45 million m³ to achieve approximately 18m depth) are subject to final verification against the approved design documents.

6. PHASE 2 – BERTH CONSTRUCTION (BERTHS 1–4)

6.1 The construction of Berths 1–4 shall be completed in two sub‑phases:

ActivityDeadline
Berths 1–2 – piling, foundation, and superstructure31 December 2027
Berths 3–4 – piling, foundation, and superstructure31 December 2028

6.2 Each berth shall have a minimum quay length of 400m and a design draft as specified in the approved design documents. Upon completion of Berths 1–2, the Concessionaire shall achieve a target initial handling capacity consistent with the investment approval (currently reported as approximately 4.8 million TEU by 2030).

7. PHASE 3 – TERMINAL INFRASTRUCTURE

7.1 By 30 June 2029, the Concessionaire shall complete: container yard development (minimum 100 hectares paved, or as specified in the approved design), warehouse facilities, administrative buildings, and all utility connections (power, water, telecommunications).

8. PHASE 4 – TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION

8.1 By 31 March 2030, the Concessionaire shall install and commission: automated stacking cranes (ASC), terminal operating system (TOS), IoT sensor network, and a cybersecurity infrastructure compliant with ISO 27001.

9. PHASE 5 – OPERATIONAL READINESS AND COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS DATE (COD)

9.1 By 31 December 2030, the Concessionaire shall achieve: completion of staff training, successful system testing, commissioning of Berths 1–4 (minimum 4 berths operational), and receipt of the COD certificate from the Grantor, as defined in the Concession Agreement.

9.2 The consequences of COD, including commencement of the Concession Term, shall be as provided in the Concession Agreement.

13. REPORTING AND AUDIT RIGHTS

13.1 The Concessionaire shall provide progress reports and grant site access to the Grantor in accordance with the reporting provisions of the Concession Agreement.

13.2 The Independent Engineer shall have reasonable access to relevant personnel, records, documents, worksites, and technical information for the purposes of certification and compliance monitoring.

14. INDEPENDENT ENGINEER

14.1 An Independent Engineer shall be appointed to certify completion of milestones, verify compliance with technical standards, and perform other functions as required by the Concession Agreement.

14.2 The Independent Engineer shall be:

  • an internationally recognised engineering firm with experience in major port infrastructure;
  • appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire;
  • independent of both Parties and free from conflicts of interest;
  • subject to replacement only by mutual agreement or by an agreed dispute‑resolution mechanism if the Parties cannot agree.

14.3 The costs of the Independent Engineer shall be borne by the Concessionaire, unless otherwise agreed in the main agreement.

14.4 If the Grantor and Concessionaire cannot agree on the appointment within 30 days of the Effective Date, either Party may refer the matter to the dispute‑resolution mechanism specified in the Concession Agreement.
