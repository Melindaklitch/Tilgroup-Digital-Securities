Executive Summary

This Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) evaluates the potential environmental and social impacts of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port development. The assessment has been conducted in accordance with Vietnamese Law on Environmental Protection No. 72/2020/QH14 and international standards (IFC Performance Standards, Equator Principles). The assessment confirms that with appropriate mitigation measures, the project can be developed in an environmentally responsible manner.

Annual Local Impact

🏘️ Community Engagement Program: 24 public consultation meetings held with local communities across Can Gio District. Feedback incorporated into final design, including: extended buffer zones (additional 50m), enhanced noise barriers, and modified construction hours to respect local customs.

💰 Community Development Fund: A $5 million fund has been established to support local infrastructure (schools, healthcare facilities), vocational training programs, and small business development initiatives in Can Gio District.

Environmental Management Plan (EMP)

  • ISO 14001:2024 Environmental Management System - Certified management framework for continuous improvement
  • Real-time Environmental Monitoring Station - Located at port entrance, data accessible to MONRE and public
  • Dedicated Environmental Compliance Officer - Senior position with direct reporting to CEO and MONRE
  • Quarterly Reporting to MONRE - Comprehensive environmental performance reports
  • Third-Party Annual Environmental Audit - Independent verification by accredited international firm
  • Emergency Response Plan - Oil spill response (within 30 minutes), chemical incident protocols, natural disaster preparedness
  • Waste Management Protocol - Zero waste to ocean commitment, 95% construction waste recycling target
