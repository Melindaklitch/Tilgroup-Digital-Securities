1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "EMP" means this Environmental Management Plan, being Schedule 5.
  • "MONRE" means the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment of Vietnam.
  • "EIA" means the Environmental Impact Assessment approved by MONRE under Certificate No. 234/2025/MONRE‑EIA dated 15 June 2025.
  • "IFC Performance Standards" means the International Finance Corporation’s Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability (current edition).
  • "ISO 14001" means the international standard for environmental management systems.
  • "Mangrove Rehabilitation Fund" means the US$25 million fund established by the Concessionaire for mangrove restoration.

2. REGULATORY APPROVALS

2.1 The EIA for the Project was approved by MONRE on 15 June 2025 under Certificate No. 234/2025/MONRE‑EIA. A copy of the approval certificate is attached as Annex A to this Schedule.

2.2 The Concessionaire shall comply with all conditions set out in the EIA approval, the Law on Environmental Protection No. 72/2020/QH14, and any subsequent regulations or directives issued by MONRE.

2.3 The Concessionaire shall also comply with the IFC Performance Standards and Equator Principles, to the extent they are not inconsistent with Vietnamese law (and in case of inconsistency, the higher standard shall prevail).

4. MITIGATION AND COMPENSATION MEASURES

4.1 Marine Biodiversity Compensation

  • Mangrove Forest: 45 hectares affected. The Concessionaire shall replant at a 3:1 ratio, creating 135 hectares of new mangrove forest. A fund of US$25 million has been established for this purpose (the "Mangrove Rehabilitation Fund").
  • Seagrass Beds: 28 hectares affected. The Concessionaire shall transplant seagrass to adjacent suitable areas at a 2:1 ratio (56 hectares).
  • Coral Reefs: 12 hectares avoided by rerouting the navigation channel. No direct compensation required.
  • Soft Bottom Benthos: 210 hectares affected. The Concessionaire shall deploy artificial reefs and habitat enhancement measures at a 1:1 ratio (210 hectares).

4.2 Water Quality Protection

  • The Concessionaire shall implement a sediment control plan during dredging, including silt curtains, real‑time turbidity monitoring, and adaptive dredging methods.
  • Discharge of untreated wastewater into the marine environment is prohibited. All construction and operational wastewater shall be treated to meet QCVN standards before discharge.

4.3 Air Quality and Noise

  • Dust suppression measures (water spraying, enclosure of material handling) shall be employed during construction.
  • Noise barriers and restricted working hours (07:00‑19:00 for high‑noise activities) shall be implemented near residential areas.
  • All vessels calling at the port shall use shore power (cold ironing) to reduce emissions while at berth.

Compensation Summary: The Concessionaire’s total environmental compensation commitment is US$25 million for mangrove rehabilitation, plus additional amounts for seagrass transplantation and artificial reefs. This exceeds regulatory requirements by a minimum of 40%.

5. ENVIRONMENTAL MONITORING PROGRAM

5.1 The Concessionaire shall establish and maintain a real‑time environmental monitoring station at the port entrance. The station shall continuously measure:

  • Turbidity (NTU)
  • Dissolved oxygen (mg/L)
  • pH
  • Total suspended solids (mg/L)

5.2 The following monitoring frequency and action thresholds apply:

ParameterAction ThresholdFrequency
Turbidity≤30 NTU (instant), ≤20 NTU (24hr avg)Continuous
Dissolved Oxygen≥4.0 mg/LDaily
pH7.5 – 8.5Daily
Total Suspended Solids≤50 mg/LDaily
Heavy MetalsQCVN 10:2023 standardsMonthly
Hydrocarbons<10 µg/LWeekly

5.3 The monitoring data shall be made available to MONRE and the public via an online dashboard maintained by the Concessionaire.

5.4 If any action threshold is exceeded, the Concessionaire shall immediately notify MONRE, implement corrective measures, and provide a root cause analysis within 5 business days.

6. SOCIAL IMPACT AND COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

6.1 The Concessionaire shall maintain a Community Engagement Plan, which includes:

  • A Community Development Fund of US$5 million to support local infrastructure (schools, healthcare), vocational training, and small business development in Can Gio District.
  • At least 2,500 construction jobs and 850 permanent operational jobs, with preference given to local residents.
  • 24 public consultation meetings already conducted during the EIA process. The Concessionaire shall hold at least two public meetings per year during construction and operation.

6.2 The Concessionaire shall establish a grievance mechanism for affected communities, with a response time of no more than 15 business days.

8. ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EMS)

8.1 The Concessionaire shall implement and maintain an EMS certified to ISO 14001:2024 (or its successor) within 12 months of the Commercial Operations Date.

8.2 The Concessionaire shall appoint a dedicated Environmental Compliance Officer, who shall have direct reporting lines to the Concessionaire’s CEO and to MONRE. The Officer’s qualifications and contact details shall be provided to the Grantor within 30 days of the date of this Schedule.

8.3 The Concessionaire shall maintain an Emergency Response Plan covering oil spills (response within 30 minutes), chemical incidents, and natural disasters. The Plan shall be submitted to MONRE for approval within 90 days of the date of this Schedule.

8.4 The Concessionaire commits to a “zero waste to ocean” policy and a construction waste recycling target of 95%.

9. REPORTING, AUDITS AND ENFORCEMENT

9.1 The Concessionaire shall submit to the Grantor and MONRE quarterly environmental performance reports, including monitoring data, mitigation measure implementation status, and any incidents or exceedances.

9.2 An independent third‑party environmental audit shall be conducted annually by an internationally accredited firm (to be agreed between the Grantor and the Concessionaire). The audit report shall be submitted to MONRE and the Grantor. The cost of the audit shall be borne by the Concessionaire.

9.3 Any material breach of this Schedule (including failure to meet monitoring thresholds, failure to implement required mitigation, or false reporting) shall constitute an Event of Default under Article 14 of the Concession Agreement, and the Grantor may exercise its rights thereunder, including the imposition of liquidated damages or termination.

9.4 The Grantor or MONRE may conduct site inspections at any reasonable time upon 48 hours’ notice (or without notice in the case of an alleged environmental emergency).

10. ADOPTION OF SCHEDULE

The Parties acknowledge and agree to be bound by the environmental obligations, monitoring requirements, and reporting provisions set out in this Schedule.
