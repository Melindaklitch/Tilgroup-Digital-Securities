1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "EMP" means this Environmental Management Plan, being Schedule 5.
  • "Competent Environmental Authority" means the governmental authority or authorities having jurisdiction over the Project or the relevant environmental matter under Applicable Law.
  • "EIA" means the environmental impact assessment documentation applicable to the Project, as approved by the Competent Environmental Authority, including any amendments, supplements, approvals, conditions and replacement approvals applicable from time to time.
  • "IFC Performance Standards" means the International Finance Corporation's Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability (current edition), to the extent expressly incorporated into the Concession Agreement, financing documents or Applicable Law.
  • "ISO 14001" means ISO 14001:2026, Environmental management systems — Requirements with guidance for use, as amended, revised or replaced from time to time.

2. REGULATORY APPROVALS

2.1 The Concessionaire shall comply with all conditions set out in the applicable EIA approval, the Law on Environmental Protection No. 72/2020/QH14, and any subsequent regulations or directives issued by the Competent Environmental Authority.

2.2 The Concessionaire shall comply with any environmental and social standards expressly incorporated into the Concession Agreement, applicable financing documents, or Applicable Law.

2.3 A copy of the applicable EIA approval certificate shall be attached as Annex A to this Schedule once verified.

3. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT SUMMARY

3.1 The environmental and social impacts of the Project shall be assessed and managed in accordance with the applicable EIA, environmental approvals, Applicable Law, and other approved environmental and social management documents applicable to the Project.

3.2 The applicable EIA and related approved documentation shall identify the relevant environmental and social aspects, anticipated impacts, mitigation measures, residual impacts, monitoring requirements, and, where applicable, restoration, offset or compensation measures.

3.3 The Concessionaire shall implement the mitigation and management measures applicable to the Project as specified in the approved EIA, environmental approvals, Applicable Law, and other documents incorporated into the Project's environmental management framework.

4. MITIGATION AND COMPENSATION MEASURES

4.1 The Concessionaire shall implement the biodiversity mitigation, restoration, offset and compensation measures specified in the approved EIA, applicable environmental approvals, and Applicable Law. The scope, areas, ratios, funding requirements and performance criteria for such measures shall be those specified in the applicable approved documentation.

4.2 Water Quality Protection

  • The Concessionaire shall implement a sediment control plan during dredging, including silt curtains, real‑time turbidity monitoring, and adaptive dredging methods, as required by the applicable EIA and environmental approvals.
  • Discharge of untreated wastewater into the marine environment is prohibited. All construction and operational wastewater shall be treated to meet applicable QCVN standards before discharge.

4.3 Air Quality and Noise

  • Dust suppression measures (water spraying, enclosure of material handling) shall be employed during construction as required by the applicable environmental approvals.
  • Noise barriers, working-hour restrictions and other noise-control measures shall be implemented as required by Applicable Law, the applicable EIA and environmental approvals.
  • The Project shall incorporate shore‑power infrastructure where required by the approved design and Applicable Law. The Concessionaire shall encourage and, where legally and technically practicable, facilitate the use of shore power by compatible vessels while at berth.

5. ENVIRONMENTAL MONITORING PROGRAM

5.1 The Concessionaire shall establish and maintain an environmental monitoring programme in accordance with the applicable EIA, environmental approvals, Applicable Law, and applicable QCVN standards. The programme shall address the environmental parameters and matters required by those instruments, including, where applicable, marine water quality, air quality, noise, biodiversity, heavy metals, hydrocarbons and other relevant environmental indicators.

5.2 Project‑specific action thresholds shall be established in the Environmental Monitoring Plan approved under the applicable environmental approvals. If any action threshold is exceeded, the Concessionaire shall immediately notify the Competent Environmental Authority, implement corrective measures, and provide a root cause analysis within the period required by Applicable Law.

5.3 The monitoring data shall be made available to the Competent Environmental Authority and, where required by Applicable Law, to the public via an online dashboard maintained by the Concessionaire.

6. SOCIAL IMPACT AND COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

6.1 The Concessionaire shall maintain a stakeholder and community engagement programme consistent with the approved EIA, Applicable Law and the Concession Agreement. The programme shall include:

  • Community development initiatives as required by the approved EIA and Applicable Law;
  • Employment and training opportunities for local residents, to the extent commercially and operationally practicable;
  • Regular public consultation meetings as required by the applicable environmental approvals and Applicable Law.

6.2 The Concessionaire shall establish a grievance mechanism for affected communities, with a response time consistent with the requirements of Applicable Law and the approved EIA.

7. CLIMATE RESILIENCE AND ADAPTATION

7.1 The Project shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the climate resilience, flood, storm surge, sea‑level‑rise and extreme‑weather criteria specified in the approved master plan, applicable engineering standards, EIA and climate‑risk assessment.

7.2 The specific design parameters shall be derived from the project's approved engineering design basis and climate‑risk assessment, and shall be documented in the relevant technical schedules.

8. ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EMS)

8.1 The Concessionaire shall implement and maintain an Environmental Management System consistent with ISO 14001:2026, as amended, revised or replaced from time to time, and shall obtain certification where expressly required by the Concession Agreement or Applicable Law.

8.2 The Concessionaire shall appoint a dedicated Environmental Compliance Officer with appropriate qualifications and authority to oversee compliance with this Schedule. The Officer shall have direct access to the Concessionaire's senior management and shall be responsible for regulatory communications with the Competent Environmental Authority.

8.3 The Concessionaire shall prepare and submit an Emergency Response Plan (covering oil spills, chemical incidents, and natural disasters) to the Competent Environmental Authority within the period required by Applicable Law or the applicable environmental approval.

8.4 The Concessionaire shall implement waste management practices consistent with the approved environmental approvals and Applicable Law.

9. REPORTING, AUDITS AND ENFORCEMENT

9.1 The Concessionaire shall provide environmental performance reports to the Grantor in accordance with the reporting requirements of the Concession Agreement and shall submit all reports required to be provided to the Competent Environmental Authority in accordance with Applicable Law and the applicable environmental approvals.

9.2 Where an independent third-party environmental audit is required by the Concession Agreement, Applicable Law or applicable environmental approvals, the Concessionaire shall procure and complete such audit in accordance with the applicable requirements. Unless otherwise provided in the Concession Agreement, the cost of any such audit shall be borne by the Concessionaire.

9.3 A material breach of this Schedule shall constitute a breach of the Concession Agreement and shall give rise to the remedies expressly provided under the Concession Agreement, subject to any applicable cure periods, relief events, and other conditions.

9.4 The Grantor and the Competent Environmental Authority shall have the inspection and access rights provided under the Concession Agreement and Applicable Law.

10. PRECEDENCE

10.1 In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between this Schedule and the Concession Agreement, the Concession Agreement shall prevail.

10.2 The Parties may agree in writing to amend this Schedule, provided that any such amendment is consistent with the Concession Agreement and does not create a conflict with its terms.

10.3 Nothing in this Schedule shall relieve the Concessionaire from compliance with Applicable Law or any applicable environmental approval. The relationship between this Schedule, the Concession Agreement, the applicable EIA, environmental approvals, financing documents and other Project documents shall be determined in accordance with the hierarchy and precedence provisions of the Concession Agreement and Applicable Law.

The Parties acknowledge and agree to be bound by the environmental obligations, monitoring requirements, and reporting provisions set out in this Schedule, subject to the provisions of the main agreement.

APPENDIX – NON-CONTRACTUAL REFERENCE NOTE

INDICATIVE ENVIRONMENTAL PARAMETERS

For information only — not incorporated into the Concession Agreement and not constituting a representation, warranty, undertaking or contractual obligation unless subsequently incorporated by written amendment.

Biodiversity Compensation (Indicative):

  • Mangrove Forest: Indicative area affected: approximately 45 hectares, with a proposed replanting ratio of 3:1, creating approximately 135 hectares of new mangrove forest. A fund of US$25 million has been proposed for this purpose – subject to final verification against the approved EIA.
  • Seagrass Beds: Indicative area affected: approximately 28 hectares, with a proposed transplantation ratio of 2:1 (approximately 56 hectares).
  • Coral Reefs: Approximately 12 hectares avoided by rerouting the navigation channel – no direct compensation required.
  • Soft Bottom Benthos: Indicative area affected: approximately 210 hectares, with proposed artificial reef deployment and habitat enhancement measures at a 1:1 ratio (approximately 210 hectares).

Environmental Monitoring (Indicative Action Thresholds):

  • Turbidity: ≤30 NTU (instant), ≤20 NTU (24hr avg)
  • Dissolved Oxygen: ≥4.0 mg/L
  • pH: 7.5 – 8.5
  • Total Suspended Solids: ≤50 mg/L
  • Heavy Metals: QCVN 10:2023 standards
  • Hydrocarbons: <10 µg/L

Community Development (Indicative):

  • Community Development Fund: US$5 million (proposed)
  • Construction jobs: approximately 2,500 (target)
  • Permanent operational jobs: approximately 850 (target)

Climate Resilience (Indicative Design Criteria):

  • Sea Level Rise: Berth elevation +1.2m above current high tide (2100 projection, IPCC RCP 8.5)
  • Storm Surge: 1-in-200 year event
  • Extreme Rainfall: 75mm/hour – 50% above historical maximum

These figures are derived from preliminary project information and have not been verified against the final approved EIA, engineering design, or Applicable Law. They are included solely for reference and shall not be treated as contractual commitments unless and until expressly incorporated into the Concession Agreement or an amendment thereto.
