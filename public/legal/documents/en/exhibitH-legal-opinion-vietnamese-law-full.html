1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

1.1 This legal opinion is provided by VILAF Law Firm (Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam) to the Concessionaire Consortium (Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A., Vietnam Maritime Corporation – VIMC, and Saigon Port Joint Stock Company) and to the Grantor (Ministry of Transport of Vietnam) in connection with the Concession Agreement for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port.

1.2 This opinion addresses the validity, enforceability, and compliance with Vietnamese law of the Concession Agreement and its associated schedules and exhibits.

2. DOCUMENTS EXAMINED

2.1 We have examined the following documents (originals or certified copies):

  • Concession Agreement No. MOT‑CG‑2026‑001 (April 2026) and all Schedules 1 through 10.
  • Exhibits A through G attached to the Concession Agreement.
  • Investment Registration Certificate No. 789/MPI-IRC-2026 (10 March 2026).
  • Enterprise Registration Certificate No. 0301234567 (25 March 2026).
  • Land Use Rights Certificate No. LUR-2026-089-CG (15 January 2026).
  • Construction Permit No. XD-2026-123-CG (15 January 2026).
  • Environmental Impact Assessment Approval No. MONRE-2025-234 (20 June 2025).
  • Relevant provisions of Vietnamese law: Investment Law No. 61/2020/QH14, Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, Maritime Code No. 45/2023/QH15, Environmental Protection Law No. 72/2020/QH14, Land Law No. 31/2024/QH15, Civil Code No. 91/2015/QH13, and applicable decrees and circulars.

3. LEGAL OPINIONS

3.1 Validity of the Concession Agreement: The Concession Agreement has been duly authorised, executed, and delivered by all Parties. It is a valid, legal, and binding obligation enforceable against each Party in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency, and similar laws affecting creditors’ rights generally.

3.2 Regulatory Approvals: All material regulatory approvals required for the Project as of the date of this opinion have been obtained, including the Investment Registration Certificate, Enterprise Registration Certificate, Land Use Rights Certificate, Construction Permit (Phase 1), and EIA approval.

3.3 Due Authorisation of the Concessionaire: The Concessionaire Consortium Members are validly existing under their respective jurisdictions of incorporation (Switzerland for TIL, Vietnam for VIMC and Saigon Port). The Concession Agreement has been duly authorised by all necessary corporate actions of each Consortium Member.

3.4 Enforceability of Schedules and Exhibits: The schedules and exhibits attached to the Concession Agreement form an integral part thereof and are equally valid and enforceable. In particular:

  • Schedule 4 (EPC Contract), Schedule 6 (O&M Agreement), and Schedule 8 (Financing Agreements) are enforceable subject to their own terms and applicable law.
  • Exhibit G (Consortium Agreement) is enforceable among the Consortium Members.
  • The tokenised ownership structure described in Exhibit A is permissible under the Securities Law (Law No. 54/2023/QH15) as a private placement to accredited investors.

3.5 Arbitration Clause: The dispute resolution clause (Article 16 of the Concession Agreement) providing for arbitration at the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) is valid and enforceable under Vietnamese law, which recognises international arbitration awards pursuant to the New York Convention.

3.6 Grantor Authority: The Ministry of Transport has the legal authority under Vietnamese law to enter into the Concession Agreement and to grant the concession rights set out therein.

3.7 Step‑In Rights: The step‑in rights granted to the Lenders under Schedule 8 and Article 11 of the Concession Agreement are enforceable, provided that the Grantor is notified and the step‑in does not violate Vietnamese public policy or national security laws.

4. QUALIFICATIONS AND ASSUMPTIONS

4.1 This opinion is limited to the laws of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam as in effect on the date hereof. We express no opinion on the laws of any other jurisdiction.

4.2 We have assumed the authenticity of all documents submitted to us as originals, the conformity to originals of all documents submitted as copies, and the genuineness of all signatures.

4.3 This opinion is rendered solely for the benefit of the Concessionaire Consortium and the Grantor in connection with the Concession Agreement. It may not be relied upon by any other person or for any other purpose without our prior written consent.

5. CONCLUSION

5.1 Based on the foregoing, we are of the opinion that the Concession Agreement, together with its schedules and exhibits, constitutes a legally valid and enforceable framework for the development and operation of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port under Vietnamese law. All material governmental approvals have been obtained, and the Project is in compliance with applicable laws as of the date of this opinion.
