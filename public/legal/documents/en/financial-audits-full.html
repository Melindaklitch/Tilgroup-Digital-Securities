2.0 Can Gio Port Infrastructure

The Can Gio International Transshipment Port, located in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, represents a $5.5 billion infrastructure investment by TILGroup (Terminal Investment Limited), a subsidiary of Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC). This 30-year concession project is designed to handle 15 million TEU annually upon full operational capacity in 2030.

2.2 TILGroup & MSC Backing

TILGroup (Terminal Investment Limited) is the container terminal investment arm of Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC), the world's second-largest shipping line with a fleet of 700+ vessels and operations in 155 countries. This institutional backing provides:

  • Proven operational expertise across global port infrastructure (60+ terminals worldwide)
  • Established relationships with Vietnamese regulatory authorities and Ministry of Transport
  • Access to MSC's global shipping network (5 million+ TEU annual volume)
  • Institutional-grade governance and compliance frameworks (ISO 9001, ISO 14001 certified)
  • Anchor volume commitment: 1.5M TEU annually from MSC services

3.1 Port Concessions 6-8% ROI

30-year government-granted port operating rights with revenue sharing. This asset class provides stable, long-term income backed by Vietnamese Ministry of Transport concessions.

3.2 Docking Fees 4-6% ROI

Recurring revenue from vessel docking at Can Gio's deep-water berths. Daily cash flow from port operations with low volatility compared to shipping markets.

3.3 Container Handling 7-9% ROI

Automated container terminal operations with high throughput efficiency. Revenue tied to Vietnam's growing export economy (projected 8% CAGR).

5.0 Risk Disclosure & Compliance

⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE: This document is intended exclusively for accredited investors. The $5.5B Can Gio Port project involves significant risks including, but not limited to:

  • Construction Risks: Infrastructure delays and cost overruns (mitigated by 15% contingency)
  • Regulatory Risks: Changes in Vietnamese maritime law (mitigated by long-term agreement)
  • Market Risks: Global shipping volatility (mitigated by MSC anchor volume)
  • Currency Risks: USD/VND fluctuations (mitigated by natural hedging)
  • Operational Risks: Management efficiency (mitigated by MSC's 60+ terminal experience)

Past performance does not guarantee future results. All investments should be made only after careful review of all legal documentation.
