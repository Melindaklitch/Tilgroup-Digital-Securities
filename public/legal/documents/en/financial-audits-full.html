1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "Concession Fee" means the annual fixed fee payable by the Concessionaire to the Grantor, as calculated in Article 2.
  • "CPI" means the Consumer Price Index for Vietnam published by the General Statistics Office.
  • "Gross Revenues" means all fees, tariffs, handling charges, storage fees, and other operating income derived from the Project, excluding VAT and other taxes collected on behalf of the government. The detailed definition is set out in Annex A to this Schedule.
  • "Independent Auditor" means an internationally recognised audit firm appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire.
  • "Revenue Share" means the percentage of Gross Revenues payable to the Grantor under Article 3.
  • "IFRS" means International Financial Reporting Standards.
  • "VAS" means Vietnamese Accounting Standards.
  • "TIL" means Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A.

2. CONCESSION FEE – FORMULA AND ESCALATION

2.1 The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor an annual Concession Fee, escalated annually by the percentage change in the CPI plus 2% (two percent), applied on each anniversary of the Commercial Operations Date. The precise mathematical implementation of this escalation mechanism shall be confirmed by the Parties prior to execution.

2.2 The base Concession Fee for the first year following the Commercial Operations Date (COD) shall be [●] – to be confirmed.

2.3 The first Concession Fee payment shall be due on the Commercial Operations Date. Subsequent payments shall be due annually on each anniversary of the COD, in accordance with the Concession Agreement.

2.4 All payments shall be made in United States dollars, by wire transfer to a bank account designated by the competent authority in accordance with the Concession Agreement.

Note: The base Concession Fee (CF₀) is subject to commercial negotiation and verification against the final Concession Agreement and applicable approvals. The escalation mechanism set out in Article 2.1 is operative once the base figure is confirmed.

3. REVENUE SHARE – DEFINITION OF GROSS REVENUES AND PAYMENT MECHANICS

3.1 The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor a Revenue Share equal to [●]% of Gross Revenues (subject to confirmation).

3.2 Definition of Gross Revenues: Gross Revenues include, but are not limited to:

  • Port concession fees collected from terminal operators
  • Docking fees from vessels using the port's berths
  • Container handling charges (loading, unloading, storage)
  • Logistics infrastructure lease income (warehouses, distribution centres)
  • Strait passage rights fees (if applicable)
  • Technology licensing revenue from automated port systems
  • Any other operating income derived directly from the Project

3.3 The following items are excluded from Gross Revenues:

  • Value Added Tax (VAT) or other taxes collected on behalf of the government
  • Insurance proceeds (other than business interruption insurance covering lost revenues)
  • Proceeds from financing or refinancing
  • Capital contributions from Consortium Members

3.4 Payment of the Revenue Share shall be made quarterly, within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter. Each payment shall be accompanied by a statement showing the calculation of Gross Revenues for that quarter.

3.5 The Concessionaire shall maintain detailed records of all Gross Revenues for a period of at least seven (7) years from the date each payment is due.

4. AUDIT RIGHTS AND PROCEDURES

4.1 The Grantor (or its authorised representative, including the Independent Auditor) may, upon fifteen (15) business days' written notice, audit the Concessionaire's books and records to verify:

  • The calculation of Gross Revenues and the Revenue Share
  • The calculation of the Concession Fee and any escalation adjustments
  • Compliance with IFRS and VAS as required under Article 5
  • Any other financial obligation under the Concession Agreement

4.2 Audits may be conducted not more than once per calendar year, unless there is reasonable cause to believe that a material underpayment has occurred.

4.3 The cost of the audit shall be borne by the Grantor, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment exceeding 3% (three percent) of the amount that should have been paid for the audited period, the Concessionaire shall reimburse the Grantor for the full cost of the audit.

4.4 If the audit reveals an underpayment, the Concessionaire shall pay the shortfall within thirty (30) days of receiving the audit report, together with interest calculated at the Vietnamese prime rate plus 3% (three percent) per annum, accruing from the original due date.

4.5 The Independent Auditor shall be appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire. If the Parties cannot agree on an auditor within thirty (30) days, the appointment mechanism shall be as provided in the Concession Agreement.

Baseline Audit Opinion: A baseline audit opinion, if and when verified and agreed by the Parties, may be incorporated as Annex B to this Schedule.

5. ACCOUNTING STANDARDS AND FINANCIAL REPORTING

5.1 The Concessionaire shall maintain its accounts in accordance with Applicable Law and the requirements of the Concession Agreement, including International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) to the extent applicable.

5.2 Within sixty (60) days after the end of each fiscal year, the Concessionaire shall deliver to the Grantor:

  • Audited financial statements (balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement) for the Project, prepared in accordance with IFRS
  • A management letter from the Independent Auditor summarising any material findings or recommendations
  • A reconciliation showing the calculation of Gross Revenues and the Revenue Share for the fiscal year

5.3 The Concessionaire shall also provide quarterly unaudited management accounts within thirty (30) days after the end of each quarter, including a detailed breakdown of Gross Revenues by asset class.

7.2 The following proposed or project‑related commitments have been identified in connection with the Project (subject to supporting documentation and the Concession Agreement):

8. ENFORCEMENT AND DEFAULT

8.1 Failure to pay any Concession Fee or Revenue Share when due, if not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice, constitutes an Event of Default under the Concession Agreement.

8.2 Failure to provide audited financial statements or quarterly reports within the timeframes set out in Article 5 shall be subject to the Event of Default and cure provisions of the Concession Agreement.

8.3 Any knowing misrepresentation in any financial statement or report delivered under this Schedule shall be deemed a material breach and shall be subject to the Event of Default provisions of the Concession Agreement, including the applicable cure periods.

8.4 Upon an Event of Default, the Grantor may exercise its remedies under the Concession Agreement, including termination and claiming compensation.

9. PRECEDENCE

9.1 In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between this Schedule and the Concession Agreement, the Concession Agreement shall prevail. The ultimate order of precedence among the Concession Agreement, Schedules, Exhibits, and Annexes shall be as set out in the Concession Agreement.

9.2 The Parties may agree in writing to amend this Schedule, provided that any such amendment is consistent with the Concession Agreement and does not create a conflict with its terms.
