1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

1.2 In this Schedule:

  • "Concession Fee" means the annual fixed fee payable by the Concessionaire to the Grantor, as calculated in Article 2.
  • "CPI" means the Consumer Price Index for Vietnam published by the General Statistics Office.
  • "Gross Revenues" means all fees, tariffs, handling charges, storage fees, and other operating income derived from the Project, excluding VAT and other taxes collected on behalf of the government. The detailed definition is set out in Annex A to this Schedule.
  • "Independent Auditor" means an internationally recognised audit firm (initially Deloitte Vietnam) appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire.
  • "Revenue Share" means the percentage of Gross Revenues payable to the Grantor under Article 3.
  • "IFRS" means International Financial Reporting Standards.
  • "VAS" means Vietnamese Accounting Standards.

2. CONCESSION FEE – FORMULA AND ESCALATION

2.1 The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor an annual Concession Fee, calculated as follows:

CFn = CF0 × (1 + (CPIn / CPI0) + 0.02)

2.2 Where:

  • CF0 = US$50,000,000 (fifty million United States dollars) – the base Concession Fee for the first year following the Commercial Operations Date (COD).
  • CPI0 = the CPI for Vietnam published for the month immediately preceding the COD.
  • CPIn = the CPI for Vietnam published for the same month in each subsequent year.
  • 0.02 = a fixed escalation factor of 2% per annum in addition to CPI.

2.3 The first Concession Fee payment shall be due on the first anniversary of the COD. Subsequent payments shall be due annually on each anniversary thereafter.

2.4 All payments shall be made in United States dollars, by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Ministry of Finance of Vietnam.

3. REVENUE SHARE – DEFINITION OF GROSS REVENUES AND PAYMENT MECHANICS

3.1 The Concessionaire shall pay to the Grantor a Revenue Share equal to 5% (five percent) of Gross Revenues.

3.2 Definition of Gross Revenues: Gross Revenues include, but are not limited to:

  • Port concession fees collected from terminal operators
  • Docking fees from vessels using the port’s berths
  • Container handling charges (loading, unloading, storage)
  • Logistics infrastructure lease income (warehouses, distribution centres)
  • Strait passage rights fees (if applicable)
  • Technology licensing revenue from automated port systems (TIL Terminal X)
  • Any other operating income derived directly from the Project

3.3 The following items are excluded from Gross Revenues:

  • Value Added Tax (VAT) or other taxes collected on behalf of the government
  • Insurance proceeds (other than business interruption insurance covering lost revenues)
  • Proceeds from financing or refinancing
  • Capital contributions from Consortium Members

3.4 Payment of the Revenue Share shall be made quarterly, within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter. Each payment shall be accompanied by a statement showing the calculation of Gross Revenues for that quarter.

3.5 The Concessionaire shall maintain detailed records of all Gross Revenues for a period of at least seven (7) years from the date each payment is due.

4. AUDIT RIGHTS AND PROCEDURES

4.1 The Grantor (or its authorised representative, including the Independent Auditor) may, upon fifteen (15) business days’ written notice, audit the Concessionaire’s books and records to verify:

  • The calculation of Gross Revenues and the Revenue Share
  • The calculation of the Concession Fee and any escalation adjustments
  • Compliance with IFRS and VAS as required under Article 5
  • Any other financial obligation under the Concession Agreement

4.2 Audits may be conducted not more than once per calendar year, unless there is reasonable cause to believe that a material underpayment has occurred.

4.3 The cost of the audit shall be borne by the Grantor, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment exceeding 3% (three percent) of the amount that should have been paid for the audited period, the Concessionaire shall reimburse the Grantor for the full cost of the audit.

4.4 If the audit reveals an underpayment, the Concessionaire shall pay the shortfall within thirty (30) days of receiving the audit report, together with interest calculated at the Vietnamese prime rate plus 3% (three percent) per annum, accruing from the original due date.

4.5 The Independent Auditor (initially Deloitte Vietnam) shall be appointed jointly by the Grantor and the Concessionaire. If the Parties cannot agree on an auditor within thirty (30) days, the auditor shall be appointed by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) upon the request of either Party.

Audit Opinion (Baseline): The independent audit conducted by Deloitte Vietnam (Report No. DL‑2026‑089, 15 March 2026) confirmed that the financial projections and accounting frameworks described in this Schedule are consistent with IFRS and industry standards. Future audits shall be conducted annually.

5. ACCOUNTING STANDARDS AND FINANCIAL REPORTING

5.1 The Concessionaire shall maintain its accounts in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) as issued by the IASB, and with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) to the extent not inconsistent with IFRS. In case of any inconsistency, IFRS shall prevail.

5.2 Within sixty (60) days after the end of each fiscal year, the Concessionaire shall deliver to the Grantor:

  • Audited financial statements (balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement) for the Project, prepared in accordance with IFRS
  • A management letter from the Independent Auditor summarising any material findings or recommendations
  • A reconciliation showing the calculation of Gross Revenues and the Revenue Share for the fiscal year

5.3 The Concessionaire shall also provide quarterly unaudited management accounts within thirty (30) days after the end of each quarter, including a detailed breakdown of Gross Revenues by asset class.

6. ASSET CLASSES AND PROJECTED ROI RANGES (DISCLOSURE)

6.1 The Project’s Gross Revenues are derived from the following asset classes. The projected ROI ranges set out below are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Actual returns may vary based on market conditions, operational efficiency, and other factors described in the Concession Agreement and this Schedule.

Asset ClassDescriptionProjected ROI Range (per annum)
Port Concessions30‑year government‑granted operating rights with revenue sharing6 – 8%
Docking FeesRecurring fees from vessels using deep‑water berths4 – 6%
Container HandlingAutomated terminal operations (up to 16.9M TEU annually)7 – 9%
Logistics InfrastructureWarehouse and distribution facility leases8 – 10%
Strait Passage RightsStrategic maritime passage fees9 – 11%
TIL Terminal XTechnology infrastructure licensing10 – 12%

6.2 The projected cumulative Gross Revenues over a 20‑year operating period are estimated at US$4.44 billion, based on the following assumptions (which shall be reviewed annually by the Independent Auditor):

  • Container handling: 32.4% of total Gross Revenues
  • Port concessions: 18.0%
  • Logistics infrastructure: 15.3%
  • Remaining asset classes: 34.3%

6.3 These projections are based on Vietnam’s projected export growth (8% CAGR), MSC’s anchor volume commitment (1.5 million TEU annually), and the construction milestones set out in Schedule 2. Any material deviation from these assumptions shall be disclosed by the Concessionaire in the quarterly financial reports.

7. INSTITUTIONAL SUPPORT – MSC BACKING

7.1 The Concessionaire acknowledges that Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A. (TIL) is a wholly‑owned subsidiary of Mediterranean Shipping Company S.A. (MSC), the world’s second‑larg container shipping line.

7.2 MSC has provided the following binding commitments to the Project:

  • Anchor volume commitment: Minimum 1.5 million TEU per annum from MSC’s global shipping network for the duration of the Concession Term, subject to force majeure.
  • Operational support: Access to MSC’s global terminal operating expertise (60+ terminals worldwide).
  • Equity commitment: TIL’s 49% equity contribution to the Consortium as set out in the preamble of the Concession Agreement.

7.3 The Grantor acknowledges that the Concessionaire’s obligations under this Agreement are several, not joint, but TIL shall not be released from its equity or volume commitments except as provided in the Concession Agreement.

8. ENFORCEMENT AND DEFAULT

8.1 Failure to pay any Concession Fee or Revenue Share when due, if not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice, constitutes an Event of Default under Article 14 of the Concession Agreement.

8.2 Failure to provide audited financial statements or quarterly reports within the timeframes set out in Article 5, if not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice, also constitutes an Event of Default.

8.3 Any knowing misrepresentation in any financial statement or report delivered under this Schedule shall be deemed a material breach and an Event of Default, with no cure period.

8.4 Upon an Event of Default, the Grantor may exercise its remedies under Article 15 of the Concession Agreement, including termination and claiming compensation.
