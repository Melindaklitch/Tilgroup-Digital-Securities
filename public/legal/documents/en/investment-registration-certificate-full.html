PROJECT INFORMATION

1. Project nameCan Gio International Transshipment Port Development and Operation
2. Project codeVN-HCM-2026-089-PORT
3. Investor (Concessionaire)Consortium comprising:
- Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A. (Switzerland)
- Vietnam Maritime Corporation (VIMC)
- Saigon Port Joint Stock Company
4. Project locationCan Gio District, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (571 hectares including Go Con Cho Islet)
5. Total investment capitalVND 128,872,000,000,000 (approximately US$4,900,000,000)
6. Equity contributionUS$735,000,000 (15%)
7. Purpose of projectDevelopment, construction, operation, and maintenance of an international transshipment port with container handling capacity of up to 16.9 million TEU per annum, including deep‑water berths, container yards, logistics infrastructure, and ancillary facilities.
8. Concession term50 years from the Commercial Operations Date (COD)
9. Construction scheduleFirst phase completion by 31 December 2030; final completion by 31 December 2040 (as per Schedule 2 of the Concession Agreement)
10. Incentives and guaranteesThe Project is entitled to investment incentives under Decree No. 31/2021/ND‑CP (special investment incentives for large‑scale infrastructure projects), including corporate income tax preferences and import duty exemptions for equipment.

CONDITIONS

This Investment Registration Certificate is issued subject to the following conditions:

  1. The Investor must comply with all Vietnamese laws, including the Investment Law, Environmental Protection Law, Maritime Code, and the Concession Agreement.
  2. Any change in ownership structure, location, scale, or investment capital requires prior approval from the Ministry of Planning and Investment.
  3. The Investor shall commence construction within 12 months of land handover and shall achieve the Commercial Operations Date by 31 December 2030.
  4. This Certificate is subject to annual reporting to the Ministry of Planning and Investment and the Ho Chi Minh City People’s Committee.
