CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL OPINION NO. TIL-2026-011

VALIDATION OF TOKENIZED OWNERSHIP STRUCTURE UNDER VIETNAMESE LAW

This legal opinion confirms the validity and enforceability of the tokenized ownership structure implemented by TILGroup for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port infrastructure investment program.

1.0 Scope of Opinion

We have examined the following documents in forming this opinion:

  • Vietnamese Ministry of Transport Concession Agreement No. 2024-089
  • Investment Registration Certificate issued by Ho Chi Minh City Department of Planning and Investment
  • Tokenized Asset Structure Memorandum dated January 2026
  • Investor Purchase Agreements and Digital Securities Framework
  • Relevant provisions of Vietnamese Civil Code, Securities Law, and Maritime Code

Reference documents verified as of March 1, 2026

2.0 Summary of Conclusions

2.1 Validity of Tokenized Structure

Based on our analysis, the tokenized ownership structure implemented by TILGroup constitutes legally valid and enforceable digital securities under Vietnamese law. Each token represents a proportionate ownership interest in the underlying physical port infrastructure assets, with the tokenized structure operating as a recognized form of indirect ownership under the Civil Code and applicable commercial regulations.

2.2 Regulatory Compliance

The structure complies with Vietnamese regulations governing private placements to accredited investors. The offering is exempt from public registration requirements under Section 123 of the Securities Law, provided that all investors meet the accredited investor criteria and the total number of investors does not exceed the statutory threshold of 100 qualified investors.

2.3 Asset-Backed Security Status

The tokens qualify as asset-backed securities under the Civil Code and relevant financial regulations. Each token holder maintains a direct beneficial interest in the port infrastructure assets, with rights enforceable through the special purpose vehicle established for this purpose.

3.0 Legal Framework Analysis

3.1 Civil Code of Vietnam (2015)

Under Articles 105-108 of the Civil Code, property rights can be established through various forms of ownership, including fractional ownership structures. The tokenized model falls within the scope of "other property rights" as defined by Article 105, providing legal recognition for digital representations of ownership interests.

3.2 Securities Law (2019)

The offering meets the criteria for private placement exemption under Article 32 of the Securities Law. The tokens are classified as "securities" under Article 4, Section 2, which defines securities to include "other types of securities as prescribed by the government," allowing for digital security instruments.

3.3 Maritime Code (2015)

Article 76 of the Maritime Code permits foreign investment in maritime infrastructure through concession arrangements. The tokenized ownership structure does not alter the concession rights held by TILGroup but rather provides a mechanism for investment participation consistent with maritime investment regulations.

4.0 Risk Assessment and Mitigations

4.1 Regulatory Risk

There is ongoing development in digital asset regulations in Vietnam. The National Assembly has indicated consideration of comprehensive digital asset legislation by 2027. We recommend periodic regulatory review to ensure continued compliance as the legal framework evolves.

4.2 Enforcement Mechanisms

Enforcement of token holder rights may require judicial interpretation in the absence of specific digital asset jurisprudence. The use of a Vietnamese special purpose vehicle and Vietnamese governing law provides the strongest enforcement position available under current law.

4.3 Mitigation Strategies

To address identified risks, we recommend: (i) maintaining comprehensive investor documentation with clear rights descriptions, (ii) engaging with regulatory authorities proactively, and (iii) structuring for potential future regulatory requirements including licensing thresholds.

5.0 Additional Considerations

The tokenized structure requires ongoing compliance with:

  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) requirements under State Bank of Vietnam regulations
  • Know-Your-Customer (KYC) obligations for investor onboarding
  • Tax reporting obligations under Circular No. 92/2015/TT-BTC
  • Annual audit requirements for the tokenized asset pool
