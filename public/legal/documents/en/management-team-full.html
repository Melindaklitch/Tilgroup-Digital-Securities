1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 This Exhibit B sets forth the management team, key personnel, and organisational structure of the Concessionaire Consortium (Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A., Vietnam Maritime Corporation (VIMC), and Saigon Port Joint Stock Company) in connection with the Concession Agreement for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port.

1.2 The individuals identified in this Exhibit have been approved by the Grantor (Ministry of Transport of Vietnam) as key personnel whose continued involvement is material to the successful development and operation of the Project.

1.3 Any proposed replacement of a key person identified in Sections 2 or 3 of this Exhibit shall require the prior written consent of the Grantor, not to be unreasonably withheld.

2. EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM

2.1 The following individuals constitute the executive leadership team of the Concessionaire for the Project. Their combined experience exceeds 150 years in port operations, infrastructure finance, and maritime law.

Capt. Alessandro Del Vecchio
Chief Executive Officer
Former Regional Director, MSC (25 years). Led port developments in Singapore, Rotterdam, and Los Angeles. Holds Master Mariner certification.
Maria Teresa Santos
Chief Financial Officer
Former Managing Director, Goldman Sachs Infrastructure (18 years). Structured over US$15 billion in infrastructure financing. MBA from Harvard Business School.
Trần Văn Nam

Dr. James Whitaker

Nguyễn Thị Phương

6. KEY PERSONNEL COMMITMENTS

6.1 The Concessionaire warrants that the individuals identified in Sections 2 and 3 of this Exhibit shall devote sufficient time and attention to the Project to ensure its successful development and operation.

6.2 In the event that any key person becomes unable to serve (due to death, disability, resignation, or termination for cause), the Concessionaire shall propose a replacement with substantially equivalent qualifications within 60 days. The Grantor's consent to such replacement shall not be unreasonably withheld.

6.3 The Concessionaire shall maintain key person life and disability insurance for the CEO, CFO, and COO in an amount not less than US$5 million each, with the Grantor named as an additional loss payee to the extent of any compensation owed to the Grantor under the Concession Agreement.

7. ADOPTION OF EXHIBIT

This Exhibit B is attached to and forms an integral part of the Concession Agreement dated April 2026 between the Ministry of Transport of Vietnam and the Concessionaire Consortium (TIL, VIMC, Saigon Port).

The Parties acknowledge and agree that the key personnel identified in this Exhibit are approved by the Grantor, and any replacement shall be subject to the procedures set out in Section 6 above.
