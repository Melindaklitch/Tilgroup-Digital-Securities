1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

1.1 This Exhibit C provides a market analysis and competitive positioning assessment for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Project”). It is based on data from Drewry Maritime Research, the Vietnam Ministry of Transport, IHS Markit, and internal analysis by the Concessionaire.

1.2 The analysis is provided for informational purposes and to assist the Grantor and investors in understanding the commercial context of the Project. It does not constitute a guarantee of future market share, revenues, or returns.

1.3 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

2. VIETNAM PORT MARKET OVERVIEW

2.1 Vietnam's container throughput has grown at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 8.2% from 2015 to 2025, reaching an estimated 24.5 million TEU in 2025. The market is concentrated in the southern region, which accounts for approximately 65% of total volume.

2.2 Key market metrics as of 2025:

Total container throughput (2025) – all Vietnam ports24.5 million TEU
Projected throughput (2030)38.2 million TEU

2.3 The Project is positioned to capture a significant share of this growth due to its deep-water capability (18m draft) and direct integration with MSC’s global shipping network.
