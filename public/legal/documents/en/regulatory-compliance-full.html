1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

1.1 This Exhibit D sets out the regulatory approvals, permits, and certifications obtained by the Concessionaire for the development and operation of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Project”).

1.2 The Concessionaire represents that, as of the date of this Exhibit, all material approvals required for Phase 1 construction have been secured, and the remaining approvals (as noted herein) are in process.

1.3 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

2. REGULATORY APPROVALS SUMMARY

2.1 The following approvals have been secured as of March 2026:

Ministry of Transport (MOT): Port Concession Agreement No. MOT-2024-089 (approved 15 December 2024, valid through 2054)

Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment (MONRE): Environmental Impact Assessment No. MONRE-2025-234 (approved 20 June 2025)

Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI): Investment Registration Certificate No. MPI-2025-789 (issued 10 March 2025)

HCMC People's Committee: Land Allocation and Construction Permit No. UBND-2025-456 (issued 15 January 2026)

Department of Construction: Building Permit – Phase 1 No. XD-2026-123 (issued 15 January 2026)

Vietnam Maritime Administration: Navigation Safety Approval No. VMA-2026-067 (approved 10 February 2026)

State Securities Commission: Digital Securities Framework Approval No. SSC-2026-042 (approved 15 January 2026)

2.2 The sole remaining material approval is the Customs Bonded Zone Designation from the Vietnam Customs Authority (reference CUS-2026-089), expected in the second quarter of 2026.

4. APPLICABLE LEGAL FRAMEWORK

4.1 The Project is subject to the following primary legal instruments:

Maritime Code of Vietnam (2023 Revision) – Law No. 45/2023/QH15 – Establishes legal basis for private sector participation in port infrastructure.

Investment Law – Law No. 61/2020/QH14 – Framework for foreign direct investment in strategic infrastructure.

Securities Law (Digital Assets Framework) – Law No. 54/2023/QH15 – Legal basis for tokenised securities offerings to qualified investors.

Environmental Protection Law – Law No. 72/2020/QH14 – Mandates environmental impact assessment and monitoring.

4.2 The Concessionaire shall remain in compliance with all applicable laws and any subsequent amendments.
