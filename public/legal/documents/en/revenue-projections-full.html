1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

1.1 This Exhibit E presents 20‑year financial projections for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Project”), analysed by asset class and including sensitivity analysis. The projections are based on conservative market assumptions and operational data from TILGroup’s existing port facilities.

1.2 These projections are provided for informational purposes and to assist the Grantor and investors in understanding the potential financial performance of the Project. They do not constitute a guarantee of future revenues, returns, or market share.

1.3 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

2. KEY ASSUMPTIONS

2.1 The following assumptions underlie the projections in this Exhibit:

  • Vietnam GDP growth: 6.0–6.5% per annum
  • Container throughput growth (national): 9.3% CAGR (2025–2030), moderating to 4.2% thereafter
  • Can Gio market share: reaching 25% by 2035
  • Tariff escalation: 3% per annum
  • Capacity utilisation: 75% by Year 10, 90% by Year 15
  • MSC anchor volume commitment: 1.5 million TEU per annum from COD
  • Construction milestones achieved per Schedule 2

2.2 All financial figures are in United States dollars (USD), nominal, and exclude VAT and other taxes collected on behalf of the government.
