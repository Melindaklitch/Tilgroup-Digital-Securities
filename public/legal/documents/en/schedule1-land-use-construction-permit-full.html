PART A – LAND USE RIGHTS CERTIFICATE

SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM

Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee

LAND USE RIGHTS CERTIFICATE

No. LUR-2026-089-CG

This Certificate confirms that the land use rights for the area described below are allocated to:

Land user: Can Gio Port Development Company Ltd. (the Concessionaire)

Location: Can Gio District, Ho Chi Minh City, including Go Con Cho Islet at the mouth of the Cai Mep River.

Total area: 571 hectares (5,710,000 square metres).

Land use purpose: Port infrastructure, container terminals, logistics facilities, and ancillary services as defined in the Concession Agreement.

Land allocation term: 50 years from the Commercial Operations Date (COD), with right of renewal.

Annual land rent: VND 15,000 per square metre (approximately US$0.60/m²), subject to adjustment every 5 years in accordance with the Land Law.

Date of issuance: 15 January 2026.

Chairman, Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee

[Official Seal]

PART B – LAND LEASE AGREEMENT

B.1 This Land Lease Agreement is made as of 15 January 2026 between:

THE LESSOR: Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee (the “Lessor”); and

THE LESSEE: Can Gio Port Development Company Ltd. (the “Concessionaire”).

B.2 The Lessor leases to the Lessee the land described in Part A (571 hectares) for a term of 50 years from the Commercial Operations Date (COD). The lease may be renewed for additional periods by mutual agreement.

B.3 The Lessee shall pay annual land rent as set out in the Land Use Rights Certificate. The Lessee is also responsible for all taxes, fees, and maintenance obligations associated with the land.

B.4 The Lessee shall not sublease or transfer the land use rights without the prior written consent of the Lessor, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

B.5 Upon termination of the Concession Agreement, the land and all permanent improvements shall revert to the Lessor without compensation, subject to the termination compensation provisions of the Concession Agreement (Article 15).

PART C – CONSTRUCTION PERMIT (PHASE 1)

HO CHI MINH CITY DEPARTMENT OF CONSTRUCTION

CONSTRUCTION PERMIT

No. XD-2026-123-CG

Issued to: Can Gio Port Development Company Ltd.

Project: Can Gio International Transshipment Port – Phase 1 (Dredging, Reclamation, and Berths 1–4)

Location: Can Gio District, Ho Chi Minh City

Scope of works:

  • Dredging of navigation channel and turning basin (45 million m³)
  • Land reclamation (85 hectares)
  • Construction of four deep‑water berths (total quay length 2,800m)
  • Piling and foundation works for quay walls and container yard

Permitted construction period: 15 January 2026 to 31 December 2030

Conditions:

  • All works must comply with Vietnamese construction standards (QCVN) and the approved EIA (MONRE-2025-234).
  • Environmental monitoring must be conducted as required by Schedule 5 of the Concession Agreement.
  • This permit is subject to annual inspection by the Department of Construction.

Date of issuance: 15 January 2026

Director, Department of Construction

[Official Seal]

C.1 The Construction Permit is valid for Phase 1 works only. Phase 2 (Berths 5–6) and subsequent phases require separate permits to be applied for upon completion of Phase 1.
