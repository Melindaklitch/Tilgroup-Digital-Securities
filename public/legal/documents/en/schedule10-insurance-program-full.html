1. INTRODUCTION AND OBLIGATIONS

1.1 This Schedule 10 sets out the mandatory insurance policies that the Concessionaire shall maintain during the construction and operational phases of the Project. The insurance requirements are in addition to any insurance required by law or by the EPC Contract or O&M Agreement.

1.2 All policies shall be issued by insurers rated at least “A‑” by S&P or “A” by AM Best, authorised to do business in Vietnam. The Concessionaire shall provide certificates of insurance to the Grantor upon request and at least annually.

1.3 The Grantor (Ministry of Transport) and the Lenders (as defined in Schedule 8) shall be named as additional insureds or loss payees as appropriate, with a waiver of subrogation against them.

4. GENERAL INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Deductibles: The Concessionaire may retain deductibles not exceeding US$500,000 for property policies and US$100,000 for liability policies, unless otherwise approved by the Grantor.

4.2 Policy Period: All policies shall be maintained for the entire period during which the relevant risk exists. Renewal certificates shall be provided to the Grantor at least 30 days before expiry.

4.3 Notice of Cancellation: Each policy shall provide that the insurer shall give the Grantor and the Lenders not less than 30 days’ prior written notice of cancellation, non‑renewal, or material change.

4.4 Currency: All policies shall be denominated in United States dollars (or Vietnamese Dong with equivalent coverage).

5. CLAIMS AND REPORTING

5.1 The Concessionaire shall notify the Grantor within 5 business days of any event that may give rise to a claim exceeding US$1 million.

5.2 The Concessionaire shall provide an annual insurance compliance report to the Grantor, summarising all policies in force, any claims made during the year, and any material changes to coverage.
