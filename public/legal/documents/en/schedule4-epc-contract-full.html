1. PARTIES AND RECITALS

1.1 This Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) Contract is made as of April 2026 between:

THE EMPLOYER: The Concessionaire Consortium represented by Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A. (TIL), Vietnam Maritime Corporation (VIMC), and Saigon Port Joint Stock Company (collectively “Employer”); and

THE CONTRACTOR: Boskalis Westminster N.V., a company incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands, having its registered office at Papendorpseweg 13, 3528 BJ Utrecht, the Netherlands (hereinafter “Contractor”).

1.2 Recitals: The Employer has entered into the Concession Agreement with the Ministry of Transport of Vietnam for the development of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port. The Contractor has been selected through an international competitive bidding process to execute the dredging, reclamation, berth construction, and related works as described herein.

2. SCOPE OF WORKS

2.1 The Contractor shall perform the following works (the “Works”) in accordance with the technical specifications set out in Exhibit F to the Concession Agreement and the drawings attached as Annex A to this Schedule 4:

  • Dredging of the navigation channel, turning basin, and berth pockets – total volume 45 million cubic metres, to a depth of 18 metres.
  • Land reclamation of 85 hectares for Phase 1 terminal area.
  • Construction of six deep‑water berths (4,200 metres total quay length).
  • Piling and foundation works for quay walls and container yard.
  • Installation of shore‑side power (cold ironing) infrastructure.
  • All associated civil, electrical, and environmental monitoring works.

3. CONTRACT PRICE AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 The total lump sum contract price is US$2,850,000,000 (two billion eight hundred fifty million United States dollars), inclusive of all taxes, duties, and mobilisation costs.

3.2 Payment shall be made as follows:

  • 15% advance payment upon execution of this Contract (against bank guarantee).
  • Monthly progress payments based on percentage of completed works, verified by the Independent Engineer.
  • 5% retention withheld from each progress payment, released upon issuance of the Taking‑Over Certificate.

3.3 All payments shall be made in United States dollars to a bank account designated by the Contractor within 30 days of the Employer’s receipt of a valid invoice.

4. CONSTRUCTION SCHEDULE AND MILESTONES

4.1 The Contractor shall achieve the following binding milestones (in accordance with Schedule 2 of the Concession Agreement):

MilestoneDeadlineLiquidated Damages (per day)

4.2 The Contractor shall provide a detailed construction programme within 30 days of the advance payment.

5. WARRANTIES AND DEFECTS LIABILITY

5.1 The Contractor warrants that the Works shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of 24 months from the Taking‑Over Certificate (defects liability period).

5.2 The Contractor shall remedy any defect notified by the Employer within 30 days, at the Contractor’s cost.

5.3 The Contractor shall provide a performance bond equal to 10% of the contract price, valid until the end of the defects liability period.

6. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

6.1 The Employer may terminate this Contract if the Contractor fails to achieve any milestone by more than 90 days, becomes insolvent, or commits a material breach not cured within 30 days.

6.2 Upon termination for Contractor default, the Employer may complete the Works using a substitute contractor, and the Contractor shall be liable for all additional costs.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1 Any dispute arising under this Contract shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its rules. The place of arbitration shall be Singapore, and the language shall be English.
