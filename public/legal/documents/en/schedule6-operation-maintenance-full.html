1. PARTIES AND RECITALS

1.1 This Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement is made as of April 2026 between:

THE EMPLOYER: The Concessionaire Consortium represented by Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A. (TIL), Vietnam Maritime Corporation (VIMC), and Saigon Port Joint Stock Company (collectively “Employer”); and

THE OPERATOR: TIL Operations (Vietnam) Pte. Ltd., a wholly‑owned subsidiary of Terminal Investment Limited Holding S.A., having its registered office at [Address] (hereinafter “Operator”).

1.2 Recitals: The Employer has entered into the Concession Agreement with the Ministry of Transport of Vietnam. The Operator possesses the technical expertise and experience to operate and maintain a large‑scale transshipment port. The Employer wishes to engage the Operator to perform the O&M services on the terms set out herein.

2. SCOPE OF O&M SERVICES

2.1 The Operator shall provide all necessary services for the safe, efficient, and environmentally compliant operation and maintenance of the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Port”), including but not limited to:

  • Daily terminal operations (berth allocation, vessel handling, container stacking, gate operations).
  • Maintenance of all infrastructure (quay walls, berths, container yard, buildings, utilities).
  • Operation and maintenance of all automated equipment (STS cranes, ASCs, shuttle carriers, TOS).
  • Security services in compliance with the ISPS Code.
  • Environmental monitoring and reporting as required under Schedule 5.
  • Customer service and billing for port users.

3. TERM AND COMMENCEMENT

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commercial Operations Date (COD) as defined in the Concession Agreement and continue for the duration of the Concession Term (50 years), unless earlier terminated under Article 7.

3.2 The Operator shall mobilise its personnel and systems no later than 60 days prior to the anticipated COD to ensure a smooth handover from construction.

4. O&M FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS

4.1 The Employer shall pay the Operator an annual O&M fee consisting of:

  • A fixed base fee of US$25 million per annum (escalated annually by CPI + 2%).
  • A variable fee equal to 8% of the Port’s Gross Revenues (as defined in Schedule 3 of the Concession Agreement).

4.2 Payment shall be made quarterly within 30 days after each calendar quarter, accompanied by a detailed invoice and supporting documentation.

4.3 The Operator shall maintain separate books and records for the Port operations, which shall be subject to audit by the Employer or the Grantor as provided in the Concession Agreement.

5. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS AND KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

5.1 The Operator shall achieve the following minimum performance levels (KPIs):

KPITargetMeasurement
Berth productivity (moves per hour)≥30Monthly average
Vessel turnaround time≤24 hoursPer vessel
Equipment availability (STS cranes)≥95%Monthly average
Container yard utilisation≤80%Daily peak
Customer satisfaction score≥85%Annual survey

5.2 Failure to achieve any KPI for three consecutive months shall entitle the Employer to reduce the variable fee by 5% for the following quarter. Persistent failure (six months) may be treated as a material breach.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

6.1 The Operator shall indemnify the Employer against any third‑party claims arising from the Operator’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct in performing the O&M services.

6.2 The Operator’s aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total O&M fees paid to the Operator in the preceding 12 months, except in cases of death, personal injury, or fraud.

6.3 The Operator shall maintain at its own expense the insurance policies described in Schedule 10 of the Concession Agreement.

7. TERMINATION AND HANDOVER

7.1 The Employer may terminate this Agreement for cause if the Operator commits a material breach that is not cured within 60 days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

7.2 Upon termination, the Operator shall hand over all Port operations to the Employer or a successor operator within 90 days, including all equipment, records, and software licences.

7.3 The Employer may also terminate without cause upon 12 months’ written notice, subject to payment of a fair transition assistance amount.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its rules. The place of arbitration shall be Singapore, and the language shall be English.
