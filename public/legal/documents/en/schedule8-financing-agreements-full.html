1. INTRODUCTION AND SUMMARY

1.1 This Schedule 8 sets out the key terms of the debt financing arrangements for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Project”). The full Facility Agreement and Intercreditor Agreement are attached as Annexes A and B to this Schedule.

1.2 The total project cost is US$4.9 billion (VND 128.872 trillion). The financing structure is as follows:

  • Equity contribution: 15% (US$735 million) from Consortium Members (TIL 49%, VIMC 36%, Saigon Port 15%).
  • Senior debt: 70% (US$3.43 billion) from a syndicate of international lenders.
  • Mezzanine / subordinated debt: 15% (US$735 million) from MSC Group and development finance institutions.

1.3 The Lenders’ rights, including step‑in rights, are governed by this Schedule and the Intercreditor Agreement.

3. SECURITY PACKAGE

3.1 To secure the Facility, the Borrower shall grant to the Security Agent (acting on behalf of the Lenders) the following security interests:

  • A first‑priority mortgage over the Project’s land use rights (571 hectares).
  • A first‑priority pledge over all movable assets (cranes, equipment, vehicles).
  • A first‑priority assignment of all Project agreements, including the Concession Agreement, EPC Contract, O&M Agreement, and material insurance policies.
  • A pledge of shares held in the Project SPV by each Consortium Member.
  • A direct agreement with the Grantor (Ministry of Transport) recognising the Lenders’ step‑in rights.

4. INTERCREDITOR ARRANGEMENTS

4.1 The Intercreditor Agreement establishes the priority of payments and the rights of different creditor classes:

  • Senior Lenders – first priority over all security and cash flows.
  • Mezzanine Lenders – second priority, subordinate to Senior Lenders.
  • Subordinated Shareholder Loans – lowest priority, subordinated to all third‑party debt.

4.2 The Intercreditor Agreement also provides for a standstill period during which Senior Lenders have the exclusive right to direct enforcement actions.

5. LENDER STEP‑IN RIGHTS

5.1 In accordance with Article 11 of the Concession Agreement, the Lenders (through the Security Agent) have the right to “step in” to cure a Concessionaire default.

5.2 Step‑in rights include:

  • The right to remedy any payment default by advancing funds to the Grantor.
  • The right to appoint a replacement operator or contractor to complete the Project.
  • The right to transfer the Concession Agreement to a qualified substitute entity approved by the Grantor (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld).

5.3 The step‑in period shall be 90 days from the date of notice to the Grantor, extendable by mutual agreement.

6. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

6.1 The Borrower shall maintain the following financial ratios throughout the term of the Facility (tested semiannually):

  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): ≥ 1.30x (Senior); ≥ 1.10x (Total Debt).
  • Loan Life Cover Ratio (LLCR): ≥ 1.25x.
  • Gearing Ratio: Total Debt / Total Assets ≤ 70% (post‑COD).

6.2 Failure to maintain any covenant shall constitute an event of default, subject to cure rights.

7. ENFORCEMENT AND REMEDIES

7.1 Upon an event of default that is not cured, the Security Agent (acting at the direction of the Majority Lenders) may:

  • Accelerate all outstanding debt.
  • Enforce the security package, including foreclosure on the Project assets.
  • Exercise step‑in rights or sell the Project SPV to a third party.

7.2 The Grantor shall be notified immediately upon any enforcement action.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Any dispute between the Borrower and the Lenders arising under the Facility Agreement or Intercreditor Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its rules. The place of arbitration shall be Singapore, and the language shall be English.

8.2 Any dispute involving the Grantor under the direct agreement shall be governed by the dispute resolution clause of the Concession Agreement (Article 16).
