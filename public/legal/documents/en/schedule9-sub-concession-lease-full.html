PART A – SUB‑CONCESSION AGREEMENT (PORT CONCESSIONS)

A.1 This Sub‑Concession Agreement is made as of April 2026 between:

THE CONCESSIONAIRE: Can Gio Port Development Company Ltd. (the “Concessionaire”); and

THE SUB‑CONCESSIONAIRE: TIL Terminal Operations (Vietnam) Pte. Ltd. (the “Sub‑Concessionaire”).

Recitals

A.2 The Concessionaire holds the Concession Agreement for the Can Gio Port. The Sub‑Concessionaire has the technical expertise to operate container terminals. The Concessionaire grants to the Sub‑Concessionaire the right to develop, operate, and maintain certain terminal areas within the Port.

Grant of Sub‑Concession

A.3 The Concessionaire grants to the Sub‑Concessionaire an exclusive sub‑concession over three deep‑water berths (Berths 1, 2, and 3) and the adjacent container yard (approximately 80 hectares), for a term of 25 years from the Commercial Operations Date (COD).

A.4 The Sub‑Concessionaire shall pay to the Concessionaire an annual sub‑concession fee consisting of:

  • A fixed base fee of US$15 million per annum (escalated annually by CPI + 2%).
  • A variable fee equal to 20% of the Sub‑Concessionaire’s gross revenues from terminal operations at the sub‑conceded area.

A.5 The Sub‑Concessionaire shall be responsible for all operational costs, maintenance, and compliance with the Concession Agreement. The Concessionaire retains ultimate liability to the Grantor.

Term and Termination

A.6 The sub‑concession shall terminate automatically if the Concession Agreement is terminated. The Concessionaire may terminate earlier for material breach by the Sub‑Concessionaire not cured within 60 days.

PART B – WAREHOUSE LEASE AGREEMENT (LOGISTICS INFRASTRUCTURE)

B.1 This Warehouse Lease Agreement is made as of April 2026 between:

THE LESSOR: Can Gio Port Development Company Ltd. (the “Lessor”); and

THE LESSEE: MSC Logistics Vietnam Co. Ltd. (the “Lessee”).

Leased Premises

B.2 The Lessor leases to the Lessee an area of 50,000 square metres of warehouse and distribution facilities located within the Can Gio Port logistics zone, as shown on the plan attached as Annex B‑1.

Term and Rent

B.3 The lease term is 20 years, commencing on the earlier of (i) the issue of a certificate of completion for the warehouses, or (ii) 31 December 2028.

B.4 The Lessee shall pay annual rent of US$8 million, payable quarterly in advance, escalated annually by CPI + 2%.

B.5 The Lessee shall also pay all utilities, maintenance costs, and property taxes attributable to the leased premises.

Use and Compliance

B.6 The Lessee shall use the premises solely for warehousing, distribution, and light assembly of cargo moving through the Port. Any change of use requires the Lessor’s prior written consent.
