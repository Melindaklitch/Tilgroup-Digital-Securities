1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

1.1 This Exhibit F sets out the technical architecture, engineering specifications, and design standards for the Can Gio International Transshipment Port (the “Project”). The engineering design has been prepared by AECOM in joint venture with TILGroup, with independent verification by Mott MacDonald.

1.2 The specifications contained herein are binding on the Concessionaire and shall govern all construction, equipment procurement, and operational systems for the Project, unless amended by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

1.3 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Concession Agreement.

6. ENGINEERING STUDIES COMPLETED

6.1 The following engineering studies have been completed and are available for review by the Grantor:

  • Geotechnical Investigation – 120 boreholes, 3,500 metres of core sampling (completed Q2 2025)
  • Hydraulic and Sedimentation Modelling – Conducted by Deltares; confirms no adverse coastal impact (completed Q3 2025)
  • Seismic Risk Assessment – Performed by AECOM; structural requirements defined (completed Q4 2025)
  • Navigational Safety Study – Full simulation for 24,000 TEU vessels (completed Q1 2026)
  • Structural Integrity Analysis – 100‑year design life, 50 tonnes per square metre load capacity for container stacking
